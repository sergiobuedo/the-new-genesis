# Methodological principles — The New Genesis (Project Universe XXV)

**Principal investigator:** Dr. Sergio Buedo Martínez  

All code changes, public telemetry, social posts, and APA 7th monographs for this project follow six working standards:

1. **One-to-one population reporting:** Never display extrapolated counts (`4M`) or decorative crowd sprites. Every reported individual must exist in `SQLite-WAL` and in the `SHA-256` event chain (`event_chain.jsonl`).
2. **Epistemic isolation and neutral timekeeping:** Do not introduce human historical or religious dates (`BCE`, `CE`, `a.C.`, `Neolithic`) into prompts or the world clock. Report planetary year and day, and keep `tests/test_scientific_purity.py` (13/13) passing.
3. **No external intervention:** Do not script discoveries or prompt agents with hints. Engine updates are limited to realistic physical or biological mechanics applied equally to experimental and control groups.
4. **Multi-seed inference:** Separate descriptive observations from the continuous observatory (`seed = 4201`, $N = 1$) from formal statistical claims based on frozen multi-seed paired benchmarks (`v2.0-protocol`, $N \ge 10$, reporting $M \pm SD$, exact permutation $p$, Cohen's $d$, and open CSV data).
5. **Explicit limitations:** State clearly in every monograph that the system is a hybrid ABM + LLM model, note residual LLM priors, and document version cuts (such as `v23.4` at Day 2,692 in the continuous run).
6. **Natural academic writing:** Write in clear, restrained human scientific prose—never robotic AI phrasing, hype adjectives, or English-style Title Case in Spanish headings.
